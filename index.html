How it works

Sign up and tell us what you need help with, or what you can teach. Get matched with a verified tutor in your subject, or accept a booking request. Then meet online — chat, call, or send photos of your work — and start learning.

Our mission

KiwiAchieve connects NZ high school students who want free academic help with fellow students who want to give back. Tutors build real teaching experience and a stronger CV. Students get one-on-one support in the subjects they're stuck on. Everyone learns something.

Need help?

Stuck on an assignment, internal, or exam prep? Sign up as a student and get matched with a verified peer tutor in your subject. It's completely free, your identity stays private, and you can book a session in minutes.
